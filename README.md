## 📖 What Is Corsair Cove?

**Corsair Cove** is a pirate-themed city builder developed by Limbic Entertainment (Tropico 6, Park Beyond) and published by Hooded Horse. The game challenges you to build a sprawling pirate haven on cliff-faces and hilltops, manage complex production chains spanning over 50 different goods, and raise a fleet to explore the high seas.

The Golden Age of Piracy is coming to an end — as the high seas are commandeered by the Crown's pirate hunters, an era of adventure and plunder comes to a close... unless you can save it. Starting on a deserted island with nothing but the wreckage of your ship and its surviving crew, you'll need to use the ingenuity of pirate engineering to establish a safe harbor for all pirate-kind.

- 🏗️ Build without constraints on cliff-faces and hilltops

- ⚓ 50+ goods to manufacture — from hard tack to turtle soup and firearms

- 🏴‍☠️ Govern thousands of pirates with unique needs and demands
- 🗺️ Explore uncharted lands for treasures and threats
- 🎯 8 challenge modes and leaderboards
